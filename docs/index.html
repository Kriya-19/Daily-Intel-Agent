🔥 Contrarian Take

While concerns about AI safety and environmental impact (like e-waste) are valid and widely discussed, the more immediate and insidious risk might be the rapid, poorly scrutinized integration of AI agents into critical everyday systems and corporate structures.
Articles highlight calls for greater AI governance and warnings about e-waste, yet simultaneously, AI agents are gaining control over smart home devices and becoming 'teammates' in startups. This rapid deployment, coupled with debates over internal vs. independent safety evaluators, suggests that 'losing control' might manifest not as a catastrophic event, but as a gradual ceding of human oversight within increasingly AI-driven environments, before adequate checks are in place.
