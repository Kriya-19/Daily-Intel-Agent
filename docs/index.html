🔥 Contrarian Take

Despite the relentless hype and colossal investments in advanced AI models and raw compute power, the real bottleneck for widespread enterprise AI adoption might be surprisingly mundane: the complexity of integrating, orchestrating, and governing multiple AI agents within existing, often legacy, IT environments. The 'failure mode' isn't rogue agents, but the insidious shadow of inter-agent complexity.
While headlines focus on breakthrough AI capabilities and massive funding rounds for chips and models, several articles ([9], [10], [11]) pivot to the practical challenges of deploying AI agents in fleets. These pieces highlight issues like data governance, unauthorized actions, and the need for new orchestration architectures, suggesting that the most pressing problems are operational and architectural rather than purely about model performance or scale.
