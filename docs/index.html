🔥 Contrarian Take

While headlines focus on tech giants' plunging shares due to massive AI investments and negative free cash flow, this is not necessarily a sign of an AI bubble bursting or a fundamentally weak AI market. Instead, it reflects a necessary, aggressive capital expenditure phase for foundational AI infrastructure and models, which will determine long-term market leadership.
The current dip for Amazon, Alphabet, and Intel is largely driven by investor short-termism and concerns about immediate profitability metrics. However, these companies are making generational investments in AI compute, research, and data centers—essential spending that will underpin future revenue streams and competitive advantage. The market is reacting to the cost *now*, but ignoring the strategic imperative and potential *future* returns of establishing dominance in AI. This capex is a bet on the long game, not a sign of a flawed strategy, and could differentiate eventual winners.
